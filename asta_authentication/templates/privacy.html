Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

AstraLearn is an independent student/portfolio project: a learning and professional-networking platform made up of three connected apps (this auth hub, AstraLearn course generation, and AI Guru). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to LinkedIn or any other company whose product it may resemble in concept.

What we collect

Account details you provide (email, username, password - stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text), profile information you choose to add (education, experience, skills), and content you create (posts, messages, applications). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email and name, nothing else.

How it's used

Solely to run the features you use: showing your profile, matching you with connections/jobs, generating courses and AI responses, and the career-path recommendation quiz. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or any third party outside this project's own three apps (which share your session only when you use their cross-app sign-in).

AI features

Chat messages and course topics you submit may be sent to a third-party AI provider (Google Gemini or OpenRouter) to generate a response. No message content is sold or used to train these providers' models beyond their own standard API terms.

Your data

You can edit or delete your profile information at any time from your account. To request full account deletion, contact the project maintainer via the GitHub repository this project is published under.
